# AI Job Market Analysis 2020-2026

## Overview
This project is an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of the AI job market 
from 2020 to 2026. The dataset contains 10,345 job postings across 7 countries 
and 6 job roles. This is my second data analysis project, performed entirely 
in Microsoft Excel.

## Dataset
- **Source:** Kaggle
- **Rows:** 10,345
- **Columns:** 19
- **Years Covered:** 2020 to 2026
- **Countries:** USA, UK, India, Germany, Australia, Canada, Singapore

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Dashboard)

## Key Questions Analyzed
1. Which job role has the highest number of postings?
2. Which country has the most job openings?
3. Do skills like Python, SQL, Cloud, and Deep Learning affect salary?
4. Does work type (Remote/Hybrid/Onsite) affect salary?
5. What is the trend of AI jobs from 2020 to 2026?

## Key Findings
- **Business Analyst** has the highest job postings (1,773)
- **Germany** leads in total job openings (7,530)
- **Deep Learning** skills show the biggest salary impact (+$15,000)
- **Cloud** skills also significantly increase salary (+$9,600)
- **Python and SQL** show almost no salary difference
- **Remote vs Onsite** work type has no major salary impact
- AI job trend **peaked in 2024** and is slowly declining after that

## Conclusion
Based on this analysis, the ideal AI job profile includes Cloud and Deep 
Learning skills, based in Germany or Australia. Work type does not 
significantly affect salary, so personal preference can guide that choice.
The AI job market started in 2020 with 1,468 jobs and gradually increased 
until 2022. In 2023 it dropped significantly to its lowest point (1,451 jobs). 
Then in 2024 it peaked at its highest point (1,504 jobs). After 2024 it started 
declining again and by 2026 it dropped back to 1,468 jobs. This suggests the 
AI job market may be stabilizing after its peak in 2024.
Based on this analysis, the ideal AI job profile includes Cloud and Deep 
Learning skills, based in Germany or Australia. Work type does not 
significantly affect salary, so personal preference can guide that choice.
